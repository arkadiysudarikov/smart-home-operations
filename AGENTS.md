# Change and review guidance

For each change, note any book principle the change trades against in the change
summary or PR description. Explain the relevant principle, the concrete tradeoff,
why it is justified here, and any mitigation or follow-up. Apply the same lens
when reviewing changes.

Consider these authors and books:

- Dave Farley — *Modern Software Engineering*
- Jez Humble and Dave Farley — *Continuous Delivery*
- Nicole Forsgren — *Accelerate*, *Frictionless*
- Matthew Skelton and Manuel Pais — *Team Topologies*
- Martin Kleppmann — *Designing Data-Intensive Applications*
- Will Larson — *An Elegant Puzzle*
- Donella Meadows — *Thinking in Systems*
- Marty Cagan — *Inspired*, *Empowered*, *Transformed*

Focus on actual tensions introduced by the change. Do not invent tradeoffs,
force every book into every review, or claim a specific passage without checking
it. If no material tradeoff is identified, say so briefly.
